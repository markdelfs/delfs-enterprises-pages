DELFS ENTERPRISES

Delfs Enterprises TV Launcher is a personal, private Android TV / Google TV dashboard app built by Mark Delfs for use on his own devices. It shows weather, sports, home-automation and personal health information on a TV screen.

The app is not distributed through any app store and is used by its developer only. Its Google sign-in requests read-only access to the developer's own Google Health data, in order to display it on the developer's own TV.
